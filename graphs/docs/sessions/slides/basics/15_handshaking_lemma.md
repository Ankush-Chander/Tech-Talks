## Handshaking Lemma

![](../../../../images/basics/weighted_graph.png)  

For any undirected graph:

```text
Sum of all degrees = 2m
```

where:

```text
m = number of edges
```

Reason:

Every edge contributes `1` to the degree of both endpoints.

Therefore:

```text
∑ deg(v) = 2m
```

A consequence:

> The sum of degrees in a graph is always even.
