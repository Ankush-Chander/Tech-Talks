# Graphs – Tech Talks

A collection of talk materials covering graph fundamentals, representations, traversal algorithms, and applied PageRank.

## Sessions

- **Graph Modelling** – Word problems translated into graph formulations (slide deck)
- **Graph Representations** – Adjacency matrix vs adjacency list
- **Representation Tradeoffs** – When to use which
- **Graph Traversal** – DFS, BFS and beyond
- **Breadth-First Search** – Detailed C++ walkthrough (slide deck)

## Notebooks

- **PageRank: Who is the Most Fearsome?** – Interactive Jupyter notebook demonstrating PageRank on a family fear-graph.
